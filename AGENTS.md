## Carpenter Digital lead-generation strategy

For lead-generation strategy, maximize unique genuine inquiries for the services and areas the client wants within approved spend and capacity. Preserve the lead quality the owner accepts. Honor explicit audience, sales, or operational objectives for work with a different purpose.

For bid, budget, creative, SEO and landing-page decisions, use a dated baseline, identify the constraint, explain the expected net additional inquiries across the account and specify when to review. Consider leads displaced by reallocation. Report actual calls (answered and missed when available), unique inquiries and spend. Distinguish phone clicks, repeat contacts, platform conversions and downstream sales. Describe missing coverage and uncertainty. A lower CPL alone cannot establish a win. Preserve tracking and verify the real contact path.
