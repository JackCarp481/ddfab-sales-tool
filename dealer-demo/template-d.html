About Us

Built by Hand.
Backed by Experience.

Third-generation welder, Jake Dawson started Iron Ridge in his garage in 2013. What began as one man and a Miller welder has grown into a 4,000 square foot shop with a crew of three — but the standard hasn't changed.

Most days we're building gates, doing repairs, fabricating one-off parts, and tackling custom metalwork for ranchers and contractors across central Texas. We're also an authorized DDFAB dealer when you need a professional flatbed install — but that's one part of the shop, not the whole story. Every project gets the same attention to detail.

Every build leaves the shop with Jake's name on it — and he takes that personally.

Custom Flatbed Truck Builds

Factory-Cut DDFAB Flatbed Kits

As an authorized DDFAB dealer, Iron Ridge offers professional installation of Double Diamond Fabrication flatbed kits — precision-cut steel beds designed for Ford, Chevy, Dodge, and Toyota trucks.

Every kit is factory-cut for exact fit and backed by the DDFAB dealer network. We handle the full build and installation right here in our shop.

  • SRW, Dually, Tacoma, and Cab & Chassis models
  • Toolboxes, bed rails, step fenders, and more add-ons
  • Full skirted and custom options available
  • Professional installation with paint matching
  • Trex deck and steel deck options
